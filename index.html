How It Works

Your guide to getting the most out of 2k2 as a member

1. Create your account & profile

Sign up to 2k2 and build your personal profile. Add a profile photo, a short bio, your interests and location so other members can get to know you. Your profile is how people find and connect with you, so make it yours.

2. Explore the platform

Browse everything 2k2 has to offer: profiles, venues, services, content, events, personal ads, gigs, products and the community forum. Use the search and location filters to narrow things down to what interests you.

3. Connect with members

Message members directly, save the profiles and listings you love to your saved items, and follow your favourites to stay up to date with their activity.

4. Book & order

Book services and venues from providers, place orders for products, and buy experiences. Everything you need is just a few taps away on your dashboard.

5. Pay securely

Use your 2k2 wallet to pay for bookings and experiences and to leave tips. Keep track of your transactions from the wallet page so you always know where your money is.

6. Manage your activity

Visit your dashboard to manage your profiles, check your bookings and inbox, and update your settings anytime. Your home is your control centre on 2k2.
