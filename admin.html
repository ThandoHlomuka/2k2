How It Works

Your step-by-step guide to managing the 2k2 platform as an Administrator

1. Log In & Verify

Sign in with your admin account. Your verified admin role gives you secure access to every management tool on the platform.

2. Moderate Content

Review and manage profiles, listings, services, venues, ads, products and forum activity to keep the platform safe and welcoming for everyone.

3. Approve Providers

Process service provider upgrade requests. Approving a provider activates their business account so they can start selling to clients.

4. Manage Users

Oversee all user accounts across the platform — manage roles, provider status and keep the community organised.

5. Manage Orders & Payments

Review product orders, tips and wallet balances. Make sure payments are flowing correctly between clients and providers.

6. Review Logs & Activity

Use the activity feed and logs to track what's happening on the platform and catch any issues early.
