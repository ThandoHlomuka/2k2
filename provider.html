How It Works

Your step-by-step guide to growing your business on 2k2 as a Service Provider

1. Become a Provider

Complete the upgrade to request Service Provider access. The 2k2 support team reviews your request and activates your provider account so you can start selling.

2. Set up your business

Create your business profile with your business name, logo and category. A complete, professional profile helps clients trust you and book with confidence.

3. Add your offerings

List the services, venues, content, events, products and gigs you offer. The more detail and media you add, the easier it is for clients to find and choose you.

4. Manage bookings & orders

Keep on top of incoming service bookings and product orders from your dashboard. Confirm, mark progress and complete them to keep your clients happy.

5. Get paid

Your earnings from bookings, product orders and tips land in your wallet. Track your balance and request a withdrawal whenever you are ready.

6. Manage your account

Update your provider settings and keep your business details fresh so clients always see the latest version of what you offer.

Ready to get started?

Set up your business and start offering your services to the 2k2 community today.

Host events, share content, run ads, take tips and grow your brand with all the tools built for service providers.
